# Bearing wear / degradation

## Symptoms
Rising vibration (mm/s) is the leading indicator, accompanied by increased acoustic
emission (dB) and a gradual rise in bearing-housing temperature. Vibration trending
upward over successive shifts, rather than a single spike, points to progressive wear.

## Likely cause
Rolling-element or race fatigue, loss of lubrication, or contamination ingress. A worn
bearing raises friction, which shows up first as vibration and noise, then as heat.

## Recommended action
Schedule a vibration spectrum analysis to confirm the bearing defect frequency. Re-grease
or replace the bearing at the next planned stop; do not run to failure, as a seized bearing
can damage the spindle or shaft. Verify alignment and lubrication interval afterward.
