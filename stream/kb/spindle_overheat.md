# Spindle overheating

## Symptoms
A sharp rise in spindle temperature (°C), usually together with elevated spindle load (%)
and higher power draw (kW). Temperature climbing under normal load is the key sign.

## Likely cause
Insufficient cooling (coolant flow or chiller fault), over-aggressive cutting parameters,
worn spindle bearings, or a clogged coolant line. Sustained overheating shortens spindle
life and degrades surface finish.

## Recommended action
Reduce spindle speed / feed immediately to limit thermal load. Check coolant flow, chiller
setpoint, and filter condition. If load and power are also high, review the cutting program
for excessive depth of cut. Allow the spindle to cool before resuming full-rate production.
