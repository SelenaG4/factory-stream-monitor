# Coolant loss / cooling system fault

## Symptoms
Rising temperature (°C) across the machine with a moderate increase in vibration (mm/s),
not tied to a change in load. Temperature drift without a corresponding load increase points
to a cooling-side problem rather than a cutting one.

## Likely cause
Low coolant level, a failing pump, a blocked line or nozzle, or a chiller fault. Reduced
heat removal raises component temperatures and can accelerate bearing and spindle wear.

## Recommended action
Check coolant level, pump pressure, and nozzle/line for blockage; top up or restore flow.
Inspect the chiller. Until cooling is restored, reduce duty cycle to keep temperatures within
the spec band and avoid secondary bearing damage.
