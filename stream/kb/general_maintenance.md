# General condition monitoring and preventive maintenance

## Symptoms
Any sensor trending toward its spec limit, multiple sensors elevated together, or a rising
alert rate on a machine warrants inspection even without a single clear fault signature.

## Likely cause
Early-stage or combined degradation, sensor miscalibration, or an operating-condition change
(ambient heat, material change). Fleet-wide drift across many machines usually indicates an
environmental or supply-side cause rather than a single-machine fault.

## Recommended action
Inspect the machine at the next planned stop, cross-check sensor calibration, and review recent
operating changes. For fleet-wide drift, investigate shared infrastructure (coolant supply,
ambient/HVAC, power quality) before individual machines.
