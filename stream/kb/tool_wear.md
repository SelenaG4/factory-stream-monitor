# Tool wear

## Symptoms
Rising spindle load (%) and power draw (kW) at constant cutting parameters, often with
increased acoustic emission (dB). A worn tool needs more force to cut, so load and power
creep up over the life of the tool.

## Likely cause
Normal edge wear, an incorrect feed/speed for the material, or a chipped insert. Left
unaddressed, tool wear degrades dimensional accuracy and surface finish and can lead to
tool breakage.

## Recommended action
Index or replace the cutting tool. Verify feed/speed against the material spec and inspect
the insert for chipping. Track tool life so replacement is scheduled before load climbs into
the alert band.
